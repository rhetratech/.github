Rhetra is a self-hosted operational platform. You model your business as an ontology of real-world objects and the links between them, then build pipelines, workflows, AI functions and apps on top of it.

### Open source

- **[rhetra-ontology](https://github.com/rhetratech/rhetra-ontology)**: point it at CSVs, SQLite or Postgres and get back the objects and relationships in your data, with every link and key checked against the rows. `npx rhetra-ontology ./data`
